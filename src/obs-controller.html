Matching

100% matches fully. Lower keeps some of the camera's own look.
These changes go to OBS straight away; they don't need Apply. OBS keeps them with the scene collection.
How it works

Match Colors takes a few small pictures of both cameras and compares their white balance, tint, brightness, contrast and saturation, using walls, ceilings, white shirts and skin rather than whatever happens to be in the shot. It only changes the camera being matched, through a Color Match filter on that camera in OBS, and only when it's confident. If it isn't, it says why and leaves the colors alone. Best results come with both cameras on manual white balance and exposure, pointing at the same room. In OBS, View → Docks → Color Match has the same controls.

About & help

BGH LIVE Controller

Version 1.0.0 (prototype) · for OBS Studio 32.2.2

Quick guide for volunteers

  1. Before the service: tap Waiting to show the title slide, then Go Live → Set up here. Pick the speakers and the schedule, check the title, and go live.
  2. Switching scenes: tap a scene. With Studio Mode on, tap a scene to put it in Preview, then CUT to send it live.
  3. Mute: the Mute button silences the audio input. A warning appears if the stream goes quiet.
  4. Ending: tap Stop Live and confirm. Stop recording the same way.
  5. If something goes wrong: the controller tells you, and you can always finish in OBS on the main monitor.
